# Snapper Keeper

Free, local-first screen capture with OCR-indexed search and a smarter
clipboard. For Windows and macOS.

- **Capture:** full screen, active window, drag-select region, timed
  capture. Multi-monitor, HiDPI.
- **Annotate:** arrow, rectangle, ellipse, freehand, highlighter, text,
  blur, crop, and numbered step markers.
- **Clipboard:** caret-anchored history popup with keyboard navigation
  and auto-paste.
- **Search:** background OCR + full-text search across captures and
  clipboard history.
- **Privacy:** no accounts, no servers, no telemetry. Open source under
  MIT OR Apache-2.0.
